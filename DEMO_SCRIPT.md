# 5-Minute Viva Demonstration Script

This script provides an exact, step-by-step click-path to demonstrate every core feature and syllabus objective of the **Enhanced Personal Finance Analyzer** during a 5-minute examination.

---

### Pre-Demo Setup
1. Open PowerShell in the project directory:
   ```powershell
   mvn clean javafx:run
   ```
2. The application will launch with the modern Login screen. Point out that the database schema and sample demo data were automatically verified on startup (`data/finance.db`).

---

### Step 1: Authentication & Security (0:00 - 0:45)
* **Goal**: Demonstrate **FR-01** (Registration/Login, BCrypt security) and **Unit 4/5**.
* **Action**:
  1. Point to the pre-seeded demo hint box at the bottom: `demo` / `demo123`.
  2. Click **"Need an account? Register here"** to show instant mode toggling with inline validation.
  3. Switch back to **"Sign In"**, enter `demo` and `demo123`, and click **"Sign In"** (or press Enter).
  4. The main application shell loads smoothly. Point out the top bar showing the active username badge and the **"Theme"** button.
  5. Click **"Theme"** to toggle between Dark Mode and Light Mode, showing dynamic CSS stylesheet reconfiguration.

---

### Step 2: Dashboard & Analytical Visualizations (0:45 - 1:30)
* **Goal**: Demonstrate **FR-15**, **FR-11**, and **Unit 2 Collections (LinkedList, TreeMap)**.
* **Action**:
  1. Navigate to **"Dashboard"** from the left navigation bar.
  2. Point out the 4 KPI summary cards: **Total Month Income**, **Total Month Expense**, **Net Cashflow**, and **Savings Rate %**.
  3. Highlight the two dynamic JavaFX charts:
     - **PieChart**: Category-wise expense distribution.
     - **BarChart**: Monthly Income vs. Expense trend comparison over recent months.
  4. Point out the **Recent Activity Queue**:
     - Explain to the examiner that this is backed by an in-memory `LinkedList` bounded queue demonstrating Unit 2 collections.
  5. Highlight the **Smart Heuristic Insights Banner** at the top of the dashboard showing active flags generated by the `HeuristicEngine`.

---

### Step 3: Signature Feature — In-Modal Live Spend Analysis (1:30 - 2:30)
* **Goal**: Demonstrate **FR-12**, **FR-02**, **Unit 1 Polymorphism**, **Unit 4 Transactions**, and **Unit 5 Multi-threading**.
* **Action**:
  1. Navigate to **"Transactions"** or press **Ctrl+N** anywhere in the app to open the modal dialog.
  2. Point to the **In-Modal Live Spend Analysis card** on the right side of the dialog.
  3. Select **Expense**, choose category **"Food & Dining"**, and type amount: `2000`.
  4. **Watch the live card update in real-time** (debounced background `Task` dispatched via `Platform.runLater`):
     - Shows current month spend (`₹12,400.00`).
     - Shows monthly budget limit (`₹15,000.00`).
     - Shows remaining budget (`₹2,600.00`).
     - Shows projected utilisation reaching `96.0%`.
     - Displays an **Amber Warning Banner**: *"Warning: Category spend will reach 96.0% of monthly limit."*
     - Shows the **Savings Goal Impact**: details how this expense reduces monthly savings and impacts the *"Emergency Fund"* and *"Laptop Upgrade"*.
  5. Now change the amount to `3000` (exceeding budget limit of ₹15,000):
     - The banner dynamically changes to a **Red Critical Alert**: *"Critical Alert: Transaction exceeds monthly cap by ₹400.00!"*
  6. Change the amount to a sensible value (e.g. `150.00`), set Payment Mode to **UPI**, add notes *"Team coffee"*, and click **"Save Transaction"**.
  7. Point out the non-blocking Toast notification: *"Transaction added successfully."*
  8. Point out the new transaction in the table with colored badge and negative signed currency (`-₹150.00`), demonstrating polymorphic `getSignedAmount()`.

---

### Step 4: Category Budgets & Visual Threshold Alerts (2:30 - 3:15)
* **Goal**: Demonstrate **FR-06**, **FR-07**, and **Unit 5 Custom UI Rendering**.
* **Action**:
  1. Navigate to **"Budgets"** on the left menu.
  2. Point out the month navigation bar (**◀ October 2026 ▶**).
  3. Point out the budget progress bars with three distinct threshold states:
     - **Green (< 80%)**: Rent and Utilities.
     - **Amber (>= 80% and < 100%)**: Food & Dining (showing warning badge).
     - **Red (>= 100%)**: Categories that have breached monthly limits.
  4. Click **"+ Set Category Budget"**, pick **"Shopping"**, enter limit `5000`, and click **"Save Budget"**.
  5. The table and summary cards update instantly.

---

### Step 5: Savings Goals & Atomic Ratio-Based Deposit Split (3:15 - 4:15)
* **Goal**: Demonstrate **FR-08**, **FR-09**, and **Unit 4 ACID Transactionality**.
* **Action**:
  1. Navigate to **"Savings Goals"**.
  2. Point to the top tracker card: **"Total Allocated: 70% / 100%"** (Emergency Fund 40% + Laptop Upgrade 30%).
  3. Click **"+ New Savings Goal"**, type *"Vacation"*, target `50000`, deadline in 6 months, and set ratio to `40%` (which would make total 110%).
  4. Click **"Save Goal"**: the app catches `AllocationRatioException` and displays a dialog preventing save because it exceeds 100%. Change ratio to `20%` (total 90%) and save.
  5. Click **"Allocate Savings Deposit"**:
     - Type amount `10000`.
     - The live preview instantly computes the mathematical split:
       - Emergency Fund (40%): `₹4,000.00`
       - Laptop Upgrade (30%): `₹3,000.00`
       - Vacation (20%): `₹2,000.00`
       - Unallocated Savings Surplus: `₹1,000.00` (10%)
     - Click **"Commit Deposit (ACID)"**.
     - Explain to the examiner that this distribution executed inside an explicit database transaction (`conn.setAutoCommit(false);` ... `conn.commit();`) ensuring all goal balances update atomically.

---

### Step 6: Automated Recurring Rules, Reports & File I/O (4:15 - 5:00)
* **Goal**: Demonstrate **FR-10**, **FR-13**, **FR-14**, and **Unit 3 File Streams / Serialization**.
* **Action**:
  1. Navigate to **"Recurring Rules"**:
     - Show the active monthly rule for Utilities.
     - Click **"Run Catch-up Now"** to demonstrate manual trigger of the background scheduler.
     - Explain how `RecurringScheduler` uses an explicit `Iterator` and `ScheduledExecutorService`.
  2. Navigate to **"Reports & Settings"**:
     - Point to the **"Enable Smart Heuristic Recommendations"** checkbox. Uncheck it to show that heuristic alerts disappear immediately from reports and dashboard, then re-check it.
     - Under **"Fixed Expenses"**, temporarily uncheck **Rent** and click **Save**; open **Dashboard** and point out the Rent-related concentration or spending-spike insight. Return to **Reports & Settings**, check **Rent**, and click **Save** again. Return to **Dashboard** and show that the Rent insight disappears because fixed categories are omitted from smart insights.
     - Show the generated **Monthly Financial Statement** in the monospace text area.
     - Point out the report's **Fixed vs Flexible** section: fixed and flexible totals and the classification beside each expense category.
     - Click **"Export Transactions (CSV)"** to demonstrate `CsvExporter` (`BufferedWriter`).
     - Click **"Backup Data (.dat)"** to demonstrate `BackupManager` (`ObjectOutputStream` serializing `UserDataSnapshot`).
3. Conclude by pointing to `logs/app.log`, showing complete timestamped traceability of every user action and budget warning.
